# realtime-streaming

A private, local-first multimodal assistant built on [Liquid AI LFM3](https://www.liquid.ai/blog/lfm3). Talk to it, point your camera, and ask what's happening around you. Nothing goes to the cloud.

- **Laptop mode:** the models and all your data stay on your laptop. Your phone connects over LAN or Tailscale.
- **On-device mode:** the models run on the phone when it's capable enough.

Status: **Phase 0 (model spike)**. See [docs/PLAN.md](docs/PLAN.md), [docs/TODO.md](docs/TODO.md), and [docs/HANDOFF.md](docs/HANDOFF.md).
